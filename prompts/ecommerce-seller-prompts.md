# AI Prompt Pack: E-commerce Seller Workflows

A curated collection of high-utility prompts for Amazon, Shopify, Taobao, and independent store sellers.

## How to Use

Copy prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or similar. Customize the [placeholders].

## 1. Product Research & Validation

**Prompt:**
Act as an experienced e-commerce product researcher. Analyze the niche: [NICHE]. List 5 underserved product opportunities with:
- Search volume indicators
- Competition level
- Estimated profit margins
- Unique selling angles
- Potential risks
Provide data-backed reasoning.

## 2. Product Description Generator (SEO Optimized)

**Prompt:**
Write a compelling, SEO-friendly product description for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Include:
- Headline
- 3-5 benefit-focused bullet points
- Detailed description (150-200 words)
- Keywords naturally integrated
- Call to action
Tone: [PROFESSIONAL/CASUAL].

## 3. Customer Review Response Templates

**Prompt:**
Generate professional responses to these customer reviews for [PRODUCT]:
- Positive: [REVIEW TEXT]
- Negative: [REVIEW TEXT]
- Neutral: [REVIEW TEXT]
Make them empathetic, brand-consistent, and solution-oriented. Include upsell opportunities where appropriate.

## 4. Ad Copy Variations

**Prompt:**
Create 5 high-converting ad copy variations for [PRODUCT] on [PLATFORM: Facebook/Google/Douyin]. Each should include headline, body, and CTA. Focus on different angles: pain point, benefit, social proof, urgency, curiosity.

## 5. Email Sequence for Abandoned Cart

**Prompt:**
Write a 3-email abandoned cart sequence for [PRODUCT/STORE]. Email 1: Reminder (1 hour), Email 2: Objection handling (24h), Email 3: Final offer (48h). Personalize with [CUSTOMER NAME]. Keep concise and value-driven.
